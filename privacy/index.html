Privacy · Updated 22 October 2026

Privacy notice.

What I collect, why, and how to ask me to change or delete it.

Who I am

AI Expert is run by Nimish Rangani in Auckland, New Zealand. I am the agency responsible for personal information collected through this website.

What I collect

  • Details you send me when you use the enquiry form or email me: your name, email address and the message you write. The form opens your own email app, so nothing is sent until you press send there.
  • Normal business records if we work together: scope, invoices and project correspondence.

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

Third-party requests

Your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and, on the homepage, a 3D library from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Those providers can see your IP address when your browser makes these requests. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.

Why I use it

Only to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and deliver work you ask for. I do not sell personal information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Who sees it

Only me and the email provider that carries our messages. If a project needs a subcontractor or tool that handles your data, I will say so in the written scope first.

How long I keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Client records are kept as long as needed for the work and for tax record keeping.

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the information I hold about you and ask me to correct it. Email me using the address below and I will respond within 20 working days. If you are unhappy with how I handle a request, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner.
